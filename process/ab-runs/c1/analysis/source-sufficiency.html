Contradictions — a human must choose

  • Fast-track auto-approval vs manual-review-only08-booking-lifecycle.html §4.4 'Fast-track': a booking is automatically approved when the berth is free for the stay and the vessel is under 120m. 15-officer-review-process.html §11.4 'Manual review only': 'There is no automatic approval; every booking request is reviewed by a berth officer before it can become Approved.' Both are stated as settled requirements (Status: Agreed / no hedge).
  • Surcharge waiver authority — Harbour Master only, or Berth Officer too13-berth-allocation-rules.html §9.3: 'Only the Harbour Master may waive a tariff surcharge.' 24-inspection-outcomes.html §20.3 'Surcharge relief': 'A berth officer may waive the late-arrival surcharge for vessels shorter than 80 metres.' The second is a specific case of the general rule the first forbids.
  • Cancellation fee window overlaps itself10-cancellation-and-changes.html §6.2: cancellation is free of charge until 24 hours before ETA (implying free whenever cancelled earlier than T-24h). 19-tariffs-cancellation-fees.html §15.2 'Late cancellation': cancellations made within 72 hours of ETA are charged 50% of the berth fee. A cancellation between T-72h and T-24h is claimed as both free and 50%-charged.
