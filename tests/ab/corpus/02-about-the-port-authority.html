Who we are

Welcome to the documentation portal for Harbour Berth Booking, the fictional port authority's berth request application. The portal collects the functional description, the workshop notes and the decision log in one place. Use the sidebar to move between chapters; every page shares the same navigation.

The port

The port handles a fictional mix of container, bulk and cruise traffic across three terminals. Our documentation team publishes a new revision roughly every fortnight.
