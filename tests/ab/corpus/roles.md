# Roles and permissions

The application distinguishes the following user roles.

| Role | Who | What they do |
|---|---|---|
| Shipping Agent | ShippingAgent | Employee of a licensed shipping agency; creates and manages booking requests for the vessels the agency represents. |
| Berth Officer | BerthOfficer | Port authority officer who reviews booking requests, records decisions and grants departure clearance. |
| Harbour Master | HarbourMaster | Senior officer; everything a Berth Officer can do plus overrides, agent reactivation and tariff maintenance. |
| Inspector | Inspector | Schedules and performs inspections and records checklist outcomes. |

Every user has exactly one role. Role names are shown in the header of every screen.
